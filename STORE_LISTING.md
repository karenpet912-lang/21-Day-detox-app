# Store listing copy — 21-Day Digital Detox

Draft copy for Google Play Console / App Store Connect. English is the
primary/required baseline — ask if you want this translated into the
app's other languages (cs, de, es, fr, ru, pl) for localized store listings.

## Short description (Google Play — max 80 characters)

> Break the scroll habit in 21 days. Track screen time, mood & real-life wins.

(78 characters)

## Subtitle (App Store — max 30 characters)

> Your 21-day detox companion

(29 characters)

## Full description (Google Play — max 4000 characters, App Store similar)

```
Your phone shouldn't run your life. 21-Day Digital Detox is a simple,
private daily companion for building a healthier relationship with your
screen — one day, one small challenge, one honest check-in at a time.

WHAT YOU GET EACH DAY
• A screen-free daily challenge (21 different ones — no repeats)
• A 60-second check-in: screen time, sleep, mood, and one win
• Habit swaps for the moments you'd normally reach for your phone

TRACK WHAT ACTUALLY CHANGES
• See your screen time trend across all 21 days
• Watch mood, sleep, and screen time move together on one chart
• Weekly reflection prompts to notice your own patterns
• A certificate — and a real one, not just a badge — on Day 21

BUILT TO RESPECT YOUR TIME AND YOUR DATA
• No account, no sign-up, no ads
• Everything stays on your device — nothing is uploaded anywhere
• Works in 7 languages
• Light and dark themes

Whether you're trying to sleep better, be more present with people around
you, or just curious what 21 days without mindless scrolling feels like —
this app is a quiet, no-guilt way to find out.

21 days. One challenge at a time. Your phone can wait.
```

(~1050 characters — well under the 4000 limit, room to expand if you want)
