# Privacy Policy — 21-Day Digital Detox

**Last updated:** September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the "21-Day Digital Detox" app ("the app")
handles information. In short: **the app does not collect, transmit, or
sell any personal data.** Everything you enter stays on your device.

## What information the app stores

The app asks you for:
- Your name
- A start date for your 21-day challenge
- An optional personal intention
- Daily check-ins (screen time, sleep, mood, habits, notes)

All of this is saved **only in local storage on your own device**
(browser `localStorage`, or the app's private native storage on iOS/Android).
None of it is sent to us, to any server, or to any third party. We do not
operate a backend, and the app has no user accounts, sign-in, or cloud sync.

If you uninstall the app, or clear its storage, this data is permanently
deleted and cannot be recovered by us — because we never had a copy of it.

## Notifications

If you enable the optional daily reminder, the app schedules a local
notification directly on your device (via Apple/Google's own notification
system). No notification content or scheduling data is sent to us or to
any third party.

## Third-party resources the app loads

The app is a web-based app packaged for mobile. To render, it loads two
things over the network from third-party content-delivery networks:

- **Google Fonts** (`fonts.googleapis.com`, `fonts.gstatic.com`) — for the
  app's typeface.
- **jsDelivr CDN** (`cdn.jsdelivr.net`) — for a small open-source library
  (`canvas-confetti`) used only for the celebration animation.

Loading any resource from the internet exposes your device's IP address
and basic request metadata (e.g. browser type) to that third party, the
same way it would for any website. We do not control, and have not
configured, any tracking through these requests — they are used purely to
fetch the file. Google's and jsDelivr's own privacy policies govern what
they do with standard web request logs.

The app does **not** use analytics, advertising SDKs, crash reporting
services, or any other third-party tracking.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone,
including children, because it does not collect personal information
from anyone, full stop — all data stays on-device as described above.

## Your choices

- **Export nothing, because there's nothing to export from us** — your
  data already lives only on your device.
- You can permanently delete all app data at any time via the in-app
  "Reset all progress" option in Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version adds an optional
feature that does transmit data), we will update this page and change the
"Last updated" date above.
